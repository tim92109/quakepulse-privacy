QuakePulse — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

QuakePulse is an earthquake-tracking and seismic-anomaly exploration app. This policy explains, in plain language, what data the app uses, what leaves your phone, and what never does.

Summary: all data QuakePulse collects is stored only on your phone. There is no account. Your precise location never leaves your phone — only a coarse regional area is used to fetch public earthquake catalog data.

What is stored on your device

  • Earthquake catalog data you sync, grouping results, computed metrics, forecasts, and app preferences are stored in a local application database on your phone (SQLite).
  • There is no user account, no sign-up, and nothing is stored on our servers.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data permanently.

Location: what is sent, what is not

  • The app can use your device location to determine your region. On Android, this is app-usage location: your precise GPS coordinates are read on the device only and are never transmitted anywhere.
  • To fetch earthquakes, the app queries public earthquake catalogs — INETER (Nicaragua), EMSC (SeismicPortal), and USGS — and sends them only a coarse regional bounding box (an area of about 50–250 km around a grid-snapped region center). This reveals roughly what region you are looking at, not where you are.
  • This is the only location-derived data that leaves the phone, it is used solely to make the catalog query work, and it is not retained by us (QuakePulse operates no servers).
  • Any internet request inevitably shares standard technical data such as your IP address with the server you contact. QuakePulse adds no device identifiers or tracking of its own.

Optional "Explain in plain language" feature

  • Only runs when you tap the button. It is never triggered automatically.
  • It sends a small set of computed aggregates (e.g. the anomaly score and signal readings, rounded) plus the grid-snapped region identifier and radius to an OpenAI-compatible narration endpoint.
  • By default this is a free AI model served via the Kilo Gateway. You can optionally configure your own endpoint and API key in the app.
  • Your GPS coordinates, your earthquake event history, and any personal data are not included in this request.
  • The response is cached on your device so repeating the request for the same numbers does not re-contact the AI service.

Notifications

Alerts (new events, quiet periods, anomaly changes) are generated and delivered locally on your device using Android notifications. No push infrastructure receives your data.

What we do not do

  • No advertising.
  • No analytics or telemetry.
  • No third-party trackers.
  • No selling or sharing of data — there is nothing to sell or share; data processing happens on your phone.

Data safety (summary for store declarations)

  • Approximate location — collected: the coarse regional bounding box described above, used for app functionality only, ephemeral, never shared.
  • No personal data of any kind is collected.
  • Because nothing is stored server-side, there is no server-side data to request deletion of — delete the app to erase all data.
